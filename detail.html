WORKSHOP FEATURE  01 OUTDOOR

Capture the Sky

A three-hour outdoor workshop where students learned to slow down, look up, and turn the everyday sky into their own photo story.

Project Challenge

Most students spend their day looking at screens and rarely look up at the sky. Many of them also think they need an expensive camera to take a good photo, so they never try.

Our Solution

We planned a relaxed afternoon at Riverwood Park. Students went on a short sky walk, learned simple phone photography tips about light and framing, and kept a small cloud journal. At the end, everyone picked their favourite shot to share with the group.

Results & Key Outcomes

  • Each student left with their own collection of sky photos
  • New friendships formed through shared creative interests
  • A calmer, more mindful way to experience the outdoors
  • Many said they now notice the sky more in daily life
